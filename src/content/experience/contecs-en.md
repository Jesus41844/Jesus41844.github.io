body: >-
  CONTECS is the Congress of Computing Science Technologies, run by UTP's
  Faculty of Computer Systems Engineering together with its students. I was on
  the committee for both editions: the first one in September 2025, and the
  second in October 2026.
---

It is a congress for undergraduate and graduate students, with talks and
workshops. The first edition was the faculty's inaugural one, so part of my
job was making it happen: setting the schedule, and convincing twenty students
to give up some of their free time to organize it. For the second edition the
committee was chosen through an open call, out of 29 proposals from
high-performing students.
