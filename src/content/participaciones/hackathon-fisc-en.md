The second one, AI Agents 2026, ran for 24 hours. I coordinated the
preparation workshops —agentic paradigms and prompt design, agents in plain
Python, CrewAI and LangGraph, multi-agent systems, and deployment— and during
the event I supported the teams as a mini mentor. I also built the verifier
agent: a Telegram bot that checked the twelve teams' repositories, enforced the
rules and, with AI, whether each team was working on its challenge. It only
alerted the organizers; the decision always stayed with a person.
